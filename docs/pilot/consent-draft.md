## Cadence rider pilot: information and consent

### What this is

Cadence is a free app that measures your riding position from video on your own computer, and keeps a record of the changes you make to your bike and how each one felt. We want to learn whether it works for real riders: whether its angle readings match a professional fitter's, how often recording fails, and whether people find it worth using.

### What you would do

1. Visit a bike fitter once (about 45 minutes) at the start, and possibly once more around day 60. While you pedal on a trainer, you record a short video in Cadence and the fitter measures your joint angles by hand.
2. Over about 90 days, use Cadence on your own as much or as little as you like. If you choose to change something on your bike, change one thing at a time, ride, and record again.
3. At day 90, download a *pilot log* from the app and send it to us, and talk with us for about 20 minutes.

### Risks

Changing your bike position can cause discomfort or, rarely, strain or injury. Make small changes, one at a time, as the app suggests, and undo a change that makes things worse. Cadence is experimental: it is not a medical device and not a replacement for a fitter or a clinician. If you have sharp, sudden or worsening pain, swelling, or numbness that lasts after riding, stop and see a clinician. [Reviewer: insurance and liability wording.]

### What we collect

| What | Contains | Always or optional |
|---|---|---|
| Pilot log (a file you download and send) | Your participant code; when you recorded, changed something, rode and decided, as hours since you started; how many recordings worked or failed and why; your symptom areas and 1–10 ratings; ride ratings; which setting you changed and by how much; the app's advice; app version. **No name, no video, no pictures, nothing you typed.** You can read the whole file in the app before sending it. | Always |
| Fitter's measurements | Your code and joint angles | Always |
| Interview notes | Your code and your answers | Always |
| Fit backup (a file you download and send) | Everything Cadence stored for this fit, **including still pictures of you on the bike and the joint positions it tracked**, your symptoms and any notes you typed | Only if you tick the box below |

We do not collect your videos. The app never uploads anything; you send files yourself.

A summary of results: how close the app's angles were to the fitters', how often recording failed, how many riders completed a change-and-check cycle, and what riders said. No names. Quotes only with your code, and only if you tick the box below.
