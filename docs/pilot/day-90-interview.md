# Day-90 interview

About 20 minutes. By video call or in person. Notes go under the participant code only, never a name. Read questions as written and don't suggest answers. Ask the follow-ups only if the answer is short.

Before the call, open the rider's pilot log so you can refer to what actually happened ("I can see you recorded twice and changed your saddle height once").

## Riders

**Opening**

1. In a sentence, what do you use Cadence for?

**The loop**

2. Walk me through the last time you used it. What did you do first, and what did you do after?
3. *(If they changed something)* How did you decide whether the change helped? Did Cadence help you decide, or did you decide another way?
4. *(If they never completed a cycle: no change, no ride, no re-record or no decision)* What stopped you at that point? Follow-up: what would have made it easy?
5. Was there a time recording didn't work? What happened, and what did you do?
6. Was there a time Cadence said it didn't have enough to go on? Did that feel right, or frustrating?

**Trust**

7. How much do you trust the angles Cadence shows you? Why?
8. Did any advice seem wrong, unsafe or confusing? What was it?
9. Did you ever worry about privacy using it? *(Follow-up: would anything change that, such as uploading, an account, sharing with a fitter?)*

**Value**

10. If Cadence disappeared tomorrow, what would you use instead? How much would you miss it?
11. Will you keep using it after the pilot? *(Record: yes / maybe / no, and why.)*
12. On a scale of 0 to 10, how likely are you to recommend Cadence to a riding friend? *(Record the number.)* What is the main reason for that number?
13. Would you want your fitter to see your Cadence history? What would you want them to do with it?
14. Anything else we should know?

## Fitters

1. How many pilot riders did you see, and how many visits?
2. How did Cadence's numbers compare with what you measured or would expect? Any you would not trust?
3. When Cadence rejected a clip or said it didn't have enough evidence, was it usually right?
4. If a client brought you their Cadence history (baseline, each change in millimetres, how each ride felt), would that help you? What would you look at first? What is missing?
5. Would you suggest Cadence to clients, for example between fits or after a fit? *(Record: yes / maybe / no.)* Have you already? *(Record the count.)*
6. Would you pay for a way to review clients' Cadence histories and send them a clear handoff? *(Record: yes / maybe / no.)* What would that need to do, and roughly what would it be worth to you per client or per month?
7. Anything that worried you, for your clients' safety or your own reputation?

## Recording answers
