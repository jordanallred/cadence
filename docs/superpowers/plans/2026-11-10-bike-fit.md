# Multi-view Bike Fit Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Deliver a free local application that completes intake, multi-view analysis, explained fit experiments and ride follow-up, including outcomes where fitting is inappropriate.

**Architecture:** A browser UI uses local video decoding and a pose worker, then deterministic measurement and evidence-linked recommendation modules. IndexedDB retains versioned sessions and adjustment history. No remote inference or paid service.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript, Vite, React, MediaPipe Tasks Vision (candidate subject to feasibility/license verification), IndexedDB, Vitest and rendered browser verification.

## Global Constraints
- Primary use is on a PC. Phone recordings are the likely common input, not a requirement; accept footage from phones, cameras, webcams, and other sources.
- No paid API or account required.
- Imported files remain on the device during analysis.
- Independent clips are separate observations, not synchronized 3D reconstruction.
- No fabricated demo measurements in real reports.
- Public publishing requires a separate user instruction.
- Successful completion may mean recommending no bike change.

## Review Focus
1. Unsupported/rotated/variable-rate video must produce correct coordinates or an actionable rejection (Task 3).
2. Occluded joints, two riders, empty frames and insufficient cycles must not produce authoritative recommendations (Tasks 2–4).
3. Symptoms outside cycling or worsening after adjustments must change the outcome (Tasks 1 and 5).
4. Browser storage deletion, full quota and incompatible imports must preserve recoverable data or clearly explain loss (Task 6).
5. Imported session text must be rendered safely and historical analysis must retain its rule/model version (Tasks 6–7).

## Shared contracts and structure
`src/domain/types.ts` defines `View = 'left'|'right'|'front'|'rear'`, `Point = {x:number;y:number;visibility:number}`, `PoseFrame = {timeMs:number;points:Point[]}`, `ClipAnalysis = {id:string;view:View;width:number;height:number;frames:PoseFrame[];quality:QualityIssue[];metrics:Metric[];modelVersion:string}`, `Metric = {id:string;value:number;unit:string;sampleCount:number;confidence:'low'|'moderate';limitations:string[]}`, and `QualityIssue = {code:string;severity:'warning'|'reject';message:string}`.

`Intake` contains rider/bike identifiers, discipline, goals, units, known baseline settings, symptom location/onset/severity/context, training changes, equipment factors, clinician restrictions and assessment-screen answers. Unknown measurements are nullable, never zero-filled. `Recommendation` contains outcome (`experiment|information|equipment-training|assessment|no-change`), urgency, observations, alternatives, action, prerequisites, recheck criteria, source IDs and rule version. `Session` holds intake, clip analyses, recommendations and append-only adjustment/ride events. Define and validate these contracts before component integration; use no untyped inter-module payloads.
